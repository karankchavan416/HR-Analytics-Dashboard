# 📌 Project Summary

Organizations generate thousands of employee records that can be difficult to interpret without proper visualization.

This project presents an **interactive Human Resources Analytics Dashboard** built in **Tableau** to transform raw HR data into meaningful business insights. It allows HR managers and business leaders to monitor workforce metrics, analyze employee demographics, identify hiring trends, evaluate salary distribution, and support strategic workforce planning.

The project demonstrates practical skills in **Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Dashboard Design, and Data Storytelling**.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

This dashboard helps answer key HR business questions:

- How many employees are currently active?
- Which departments have the highest workforce?
- What are the hiring and termination trends?
- How are employees distributed across different locations?
- What is the salary distribution across education levels?
- How does employee age relate to salary?
- How is workforce performance distributed?
- What is the gender composition of the organization?
